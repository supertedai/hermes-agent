# ADR-EFC-ATLAS-001 — Separate EFC theory, meta-model and ASI-system use

**Status:** Proposed / owner review required  
**Parent:** `MWP-UOSH-001`

## Context

EFC has a large DOI-indexed development corpus and is described as a framework
spanning ontology, meta-science, field/topology, regimes, dynamics and
cognition/AI. The complete BL-141 graph readback shows 167 DOI records, mostly
Morten-authored, with zero graph evidence counts. The corpus therefore cannot
be treated as 167 independent confirmations.

## Decision

EFC will be integrated on three controlled tracks:

1. **Theory track:** cosmology, consciousness and physical claims remain
   hypothesis/evidence/prediction artifacts with their own scientific gates.
2. **Meta-model track:** EFC may organize models, regimes, measurement validity,
   entropy/uncertainty and cross-domain relationships as derived metadata.
3. **ASI-system track:** EFC may be tested as an attention/routing/evaluation
   lens for Cortex, world model, Jetstream and agents.

The meta-model and ASI tracks may be evaluated without accepting the cosmological
track as true.

## Hard boundaries

- No atlas coordinate is a truth ranking.
- No entropy score is accepted without type, measurement method, calibration and
  provenance.
- Self-placement of EFC is a proposal requiring adversarial placement and
  independent evaluation.
- Internal EFC documents do not count as independent external evidence.
- No EFC-derived signal gains graph, memory, skill, scheduler or runtime
  authority without MWP gates.

## Acceptance tests

```text
EFC routing vs baseline routing
EFC atlas retrieval vs ordinary metadata retrieval
EFC entropy prioritization vs static prioritization
EFC model-placement calibration
cross-domain transfer
ablation of EFC signals
independent reviewer evaluation
```
